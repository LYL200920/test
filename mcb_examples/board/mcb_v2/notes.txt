MDIO can't be implemented with CMT because CMT's period is fixed and it
needs a 100/200 ns period.







The MSTPC19 bit should be rewritten while the oscillation of the clock to be
controlled by this bit is stable. For entering software
standby mode after writing a new value to this bit, wait for two cycles of
the slowest clock among the clocks output by the
oscillators actually oscillating, and then execute a WAIT instruction.
